=====================================
   VIDEO FILES GO HERE
=====================================

Drop your .mp4 video clips into this folder.

Then open script.js and update the video paths in the
CONTENT_DATA array near the top of the file.

Example:
  If you drop "our_trip.mp4" here, set the videoSrc to:
    videoSrc: "videos/our_trip.mp4"

Supported formats: .mp4 (recommended), .webm, .ogg
